Questions, answered

Do I need an X account?

No. VideoFeed works without an account. Enter a public username and the app does the rest.

Where do the videos come from?

VideoFeed fetches videos from public Nitter instances. Because these instances are run by third parties, loading speed and availability can vary.

How do I install the APK?

Download the file on your Android phone and open it. If Android asks, allow installs from your browser or file manager for this one time, then tap Install.

Is VideoFeed affiliated with X?

No. VideoFeed is an independent app and is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by X Corp. X is a trademark of its respective owner.
