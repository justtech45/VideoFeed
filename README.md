## About

**VideoFeed** is an open-source application that delivers a smooth, distraction-free, vertical-style video browsing experience. It is built with privacy and performance in mind, drawing its core inspiration and foundation from the [Nitter](https://github.com/zedeus/nitter) project — a free and open-source alternative front-end focused on privacy and speed.

The goal is simple: **watch video content without the clutter** — no ads, no tracking, and no account required.

## Features

- 🎥 **Seamless video feed** — smooth, continuous browsing built for video-first content
- 🔒 **Privacy-focused** — inspired by Nitter's approach: no trackers, no ads, no account needed
- ⚡ **Fast and lightweight** — minimal interface that stays out of your way
- 🎨 **Clean, modern UI** — designed to keep the focus on the content
- 📱 **Responsive design** — comfortable on phones, tablets, and desktops
- 🧩 **Open source** — free to use, study, modify, and improve under the MIT License
